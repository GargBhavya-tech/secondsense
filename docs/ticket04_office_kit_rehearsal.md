# Ticket #4 — Office Kit Workflow Rehearsed (pre-event)

**Phase 0 · Pre-event · Prototype · Must-build before doors open**
Office Kit usage = **10% of score, measured from device telemetry.** Speed is scored. Not a day-of thing.

Office Kit (pc.vivoglobal.com) is vivo's phone↔laptop bridge. During Red Light it lets you use the
laptop's keyboard/screen while all computation stays on the phone — that's the loophole that makes
Red Light bearable without breaking the rule.

## Practice loop (every team member, until reflexive)
- [ ] **Screen mirror** — phone screen live on the laptop
- [ ] **Remote control** — drive the phone from laptop keyboard/mouse
- [ ] **Clipboard sync** — copy on one device, paste on the other
- [ ] **File transfer** — drag a file both directions

## Timed target
- [ ] Cold start: mirror + push a file both ways + drive the phone — **all under a minute**
- [ ] Every team member can do it without hesitation

## Habit to maintain (passive points)
- [ ] Keep the screen-mirror **running throughout every Red-Light session** — HackTracker scores the
      connected-usage automatically. Idle-but-mirrored beats not-mirrored.

**Done when:** the whole team runs the four-part loop fast and cold, and screen-mirror-always-on is a habit.
